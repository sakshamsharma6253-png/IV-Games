Quick Ludo Guide

Objective
Be the first player to move all 4 of your tokens from your base to the home.
Starting
You must roll a 6 on the dice to move a token out of your base and onto the starting square.
Movement
Roll the dice and move your active token forward by the exact number shown.
Extra Turn
Rolling a 6 gives you an additional dice roll.
Capturing
If your token lands on an opponent's token, their token is sent back to base, and you get an extra roll.

Interactive Tips

Try clicking on your tokens to move them when it's your turn. Select the Game Mode from the dropdown before rolling the dice!
