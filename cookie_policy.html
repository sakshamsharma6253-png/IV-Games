2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer, smartphone, tablet, or other device when you visit a website.
Cookies allow websites to recognize your device, remember preferences, improve performance, enhance security, and provide a better browsing experience.

Cookies generally do not contain information that directly identifies an individual. However, certain information collected through cookies may be linked with other information held by third-party service providers.

3. Why We Use Cookies

  • Ensuring the proper operation of the Website.

  • Maintaining website security.
  • Understanding how visitors interact with the Website.
  • Measuring website traffic and usage statistics.
  • Delivering advertisements through Google AdSense.
  • Enhancing user experience across different devices and browsers.

We do not use cookies to request or intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or postal address.
