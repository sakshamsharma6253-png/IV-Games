Play Guide

The Goal
Survive as long as possible! Dodge incoming buses, jump over barricades, and slide under signs. The longer you run, the faster the game gets. Collect coins to boost your high score.
Controls
You need quick reflexes to stay alive. Use the following keys:
  • Up Arrow / W: Jump over low obstacles.
  • Down Arrow / S: Slide under high obstacles.
  • Left/Right Arrows / A & D: Switch lanes to dodge buses and collect points.
Tips & Tricks
Surviving at high speeds requires strategy. Keep these in mind:
  • Look Ahead: Don't focus on your character; keep your eyes on the horizon to spot incoming buses early.
  • Stay Centered: When possible, try to run in the middle lane. It gives you the fastest escape route to either the left or right side.
  • Speed Increments: As your score goes up, the game speed increases. Stay calm, don't panic, and trust your reflexes!
