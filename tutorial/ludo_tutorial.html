Quick Ludo Guide

Objective
Be the first player to move all 4 of your tokens from your base to the home in the center of the board.
Starting
You must roll a 6 on the dice to move a token out of your base and onto the starting square.
Movement
Roll the dice and move your active token forward by the exact number shown. Your tokens move in a clockwise direction around the board.
Extra Turn
Rolling a 6 gives you an additional dice roll! You can either open a new token or advance an active one.
Capturing
If your token lands exactly on the same square as an opponent's token, their token is captured and sent back to base! Plus, you get an extra roll as a reward.

Interactive Tips

In our version, you don't need a "Roll Dice" button. The game rolls automatically when it's your turn, and you simply click on the token you want to move. Select the Game Mode from the dropdown before starting!
