Quick Chess Guide

Pieces
King, Queen, Rook, Bishop, Knight, Pawn — each moves differently.
Objective
Checkmate the opponent's king (trap it so it cannot escape check).
Turns
White moves first; players alternate moves.
AI
Choose Easy for random moves; Hard runs a short minimax to plan ahead.

Interactive Tips

Use Undo to step back, and export PGN to review games later.
