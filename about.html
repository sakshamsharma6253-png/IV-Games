About IV Games

About IT Voice

IV Games is the official gaming platform developed and operated by IT Voice Media Private Limited, a leading Indian technology media organization dedicated to delivering technology news, industry insights, digital publications, and technology events. For years, IT Voice has served the technology ecosystem by connecting businesses, professionals, and technology enthusiasts through reliable journalism, industry coverage, print and digital publications, interviews, and technology events. With IV Games, we extend our commitment to technology into the world of digital entertainment.

Our Vision

Technology is not only about work—it is also about recreation, learning, creativity, and relaxation. IV Games has been created to provide users with a simple, accessible, and enjoyable gaming experience that anyone can enjoy directly from their browser without unnecessary complexity. Our mission is to combine modern web technologies with engaging casual games that offer entertainment during breaks, leisure time, or moments of relaxation.

What We Offer

IV Games provides browser-based games that are easy to access without requiring downloads or software installation. Our collection currently includes games such as:

  • Bus Runner – Dodge obstacles, collect rewards, and run as far as you can.
  • Chess – Challenge your strategic thinking with the timeless game of chess.
  • Ludo – Enjoy the classic multiplayer board game with family and friends.
  • Tic-Tac-Toe – A simple yet exciting game of quick thinking.
  • Mini Sudoku – Sharpen your logical reasoning with number puzzles.
  • N-Queens – Solve the famous chess puzzle by placing queens without conflicts.

We will continue expanding our library by introducing new games, improved gameplay mechanics, enhanced graphics, and additional entertainment experiences.
